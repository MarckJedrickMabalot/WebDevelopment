Superhero Dispatch Network
Dispatch is a superhero workplace comedy game. You play Robert Robertson, a retired hero who now runs dispatch, sending a team of reformed villains out on calls across Los Angeles. Every hero has their own stats and quirks, so choose who you send wisely. Search the database below to pull up their files.
